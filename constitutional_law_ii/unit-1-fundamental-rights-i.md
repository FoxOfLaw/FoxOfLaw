# CONSTITUTIONAL LAW – II

## UNIT I — FUNDAMENTAL RIGHTS – I

- Fundamental Rights are in Part III (Articles 12–35) and are enforceable by courts.
- Evolution: colonial experience, freedom movement, Nehru Report, Constituent Assembly debates.

### Article 12 — Definition of State

- Includes: Government of India, Parliament, State Governments, State Legislatures, local authorities, and other authorities under Government control
- Instrumentality/Agency Doctrine: courts extend Article 12 to bodies functioning as government instruments (R.D. Shetty; Ajay Hasia)

### Article 13

- Constitutional supremacy over inconsistent laws; foundation for judicial review
- Doctrines: severability (reading down), eclipse (pre-constitution laws rendered unenforceable to extent of inconsistency)
