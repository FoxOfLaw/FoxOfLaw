# UNIT II — FUNDAMENTAL RIGHTS II

## ARTICLE 14 — Equality Before Law

- Article 14: equality before law and equal protection of laws
- Negative concept: equality before law (no one above law)
- Positive concept: equal protection (similarly situated treated similarly)

### Reasonable Classification

- Intelligible differentia
- Rational nexus between differentia and legislative objective

### Modern Doctrine

- Article 14 prohibits arbitrariness (E.P. Royappa)
- Maneka Gandhi: Art.14 read with Arts.19 and 21
- Compensatory discrimination allowed (Articles 15 & 16 provisions)

## ARTICLE 19 — Freedoms

- Six major freedoms for citizens: speech/expression; peaceful assembly; association; movement; residence; profession
- Freedom of speech includes press, political expression, artistic and online expression; subject to reasonable restrictions under Art.19(2)
- Important cases: Romesh Thappar; Bennett Coleman; Shreya Singhal

## ARTICLE 21 — Life and Personal Liberty

- "No person shall be deprived of life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law."
- Maneka Gandhi: procedure must be fair, just and reasonable
- Rights read into Art.21: privacy, livelihood, shelter, clean environment, legal aid, speedy trial, dignity, protection against custodial violence
- Important cases: Francis Coralie Mullin; Olga Tellis; D.K. Basu; K.S. Puttaswamy

## Other Articles

- Article 21A: Right to Education (86th Amendment; RTE Act, 2009)
- Article 22: safeguards on arrest/detention; preventive detention subject to safeguards
- Article 23: prohibition of trafficking and forced labour (PUDR; Bandhua Mukti Morcha)
- Article 24: child labour protections
- Article 25–28: freedom of religion and associated doctrines (Shirur Mutt)
- Articles 29–30: minority rights (T.M.A. Pai; P.A. Inamdar)
