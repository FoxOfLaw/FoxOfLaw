# UNIT IV — DIRECTIVE PRINCIPLES AND FUNDAMENTAL DUTIES

## Directive Principles of State Policy (Articles 36–51)

- Not enforceable by courts but fundamental in governance (Art.37)
- Classification: Socialist principles (Arts.38,39,39A,41,42,43); Gandhian principles (Arts.40,43,46,47,48); Liberal-Intellectual (Arts.44,45,48A,49,50,51)

## Fundamental Rights vs DPSP

- Initially courts held FRs prevailed (Champakam Dorairajan)
- Later jurisprudence (Kesavananda, Minerva Mills) balanced FRs and DPSPs; harmony between Parts III & IV is part of basic structure

## Fundamental Duties (Article 51A)

- Introduced by 42nd Amendment (1976); additional duty added by 86th Amendment regarding education
- Duties: respect Constitution, national symbols, protect sovereignty/integrity, promote harmony, protect environment, develop scientific temper, safeguard public property, provide education to children
- Courts may use duties in interpretation and to strengthen protections (e.g., M.C. Mehta on environment)
