# UNIT III — RIGHT TO CONSTITUTIONAL REMEDIES

## Article 32 and Article 226

- Article 32: enforcement of Fundamental Rights at the Supreme Court — "heart and soul" of the Constitution (Ambedkar)
- Article 226: High Courts can issue writs for Fundamental Rights and other legal rights (broader than Art.32)

## Five Constitutional Writs

1. Habeas Corpus — challenge unlawful detention; "you may have the body"
2. Mandamus — command to public authority to perform legal duty
3. Certiorari — quash orders of lower courts/tribunals for jurisdictional error or error of law
4. Prohibition — prevent lower court/tribunal from exceeding jurisdiction
5. Quo Warranto — challenge illegal occupation of public office

## Public Interest Litigation (PIL)

- Enables litigation in public interest where affected persons cannot approach courts
- Important cases: S.P. Gupta; Bandhua Mukti Morcha; PUDR
